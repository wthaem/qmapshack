## Layers

The map view draws independent layers.

- Maps, elevation data and routing data are not linked. Elevation data works with any map.
- Activate each one in its own list.


## Items

Click an item or open its context menu to get its actions.

## Workspace and database

- The database stores your data.
- To change data, load it from the database into the workspace.
